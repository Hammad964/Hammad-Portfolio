About me

I care about what happens after the app ships.

I’m a Native Mobile Application Developer based in Karachi, Pakistan, with 5+ years of professional experience building iOS and Android applications. My recent work is focused primarily on iOS development, while my broader background includes professional Android development across client and product projects.

I’ve worked on fintech, banking, e-commerce, enterprise and engagement applications involving secure APIs, payments, authentication, Firebase, local persistence, biometrics, maps, third-party SDKs and production releases.

My focus is straightforward: maintainable architecture, practical security, reliable integrations and mobile experiences that hold up in production.

01

Build for production

Architecture, error handling, release discipline and maintainability matter as much as feature delivery.

02

Security is part of the feature

SSL pinning, secure storage, biometrics, encryption and authentication are built into the workflow—not added later.

03

Keep the user flow simple

Complex business logic should still result in an interface that feels predictable, responsive and easy to use.
